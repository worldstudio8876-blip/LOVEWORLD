# LOVEWORLD — YouTube channel workspace

Read this first in every session. It holds the locked decisions for the channels we produce.

## Channels

- **Erich Fromm – Die Kunst zu Leben** (new, German). Full foundation in
  `channels/erich-fromm/channel-foundation.md`. That document is the source of truth for
  identity, audience, format, the three script templates, title/description/hashtag/playlist
  rules and thumbnail rules.
- **Carl Jung** (sister channel, existing). Content and ideas are shared between the two channels.
  Its foundation document has not been added to this repo yet. Add it under `channels/carl-jung/`.

## Rules for every Fromm script

1. Pick one of the three templates: A (sleep/hypnotic, 2.5–4 h), B (standard single topic,
   30–45 min) or C (manifestation ritual, scarcity/urgency). Follow its beat structure.
2. **Scripts from the Carl Jung channel:** when the user gives a Jung script, do NOT write a new
   script. Keep the text word for word and only change the name Carl/Karl Jung to Erich Fromm.
   Allowed cleanup only: remove transcript markers like [musik] and fix obvious transcription
   typos. The rest of the package (title, description, hashtags, thumbnail, playlist) is still
   built around it.
3. **Language:** scripts are in English by default. Write in German only when the user says
   that script is German. German scripts always use Du-Form. Plan length at ~119–128 words per
   minute. Title, description, hashtags and thumbnail text follow the script's language.
4. Deliver the full package: title, description (the title verbatim), hashtags, thumbnail concept,
   playlist ("Die Philosophie von Erich Fromm"), the script, plus PDF and TXT files.
5. Do not invent quotes and attribute them to Fromm or Jung. Mark any quote that has not been
   checked against the primary text.

## Thumbnails

- Generate with **Buzzy** (`mcp__Buzzy__GenerateImage`), 16:9.
- Fromm's face must match the locked likeness photos in `channels/erich-fromm/reference/`
  (`fromm-portrait-01..04`). Upload one or more as image references on every generation.
- Style reference: `channels/erich-fromm/reference/thumbnail-style-reference.webp`. Fully
  desaturated B&W portrait on the left, navy starfield with a teal nebula tint, 2–4 lines of
  bold ALL-CAPS text with one phrase in yellow or on a red box, small topic icon in a bottom
  corner, no duration stamp.
- The thumbnail text paraphrases the hook. It never repeats the video title.
- The pose matches the topic's mood (pensive, shush, praying hands, alarmed stare, side profile).
