## Narration (one line per 10-second block)

The numbers are spelled out on purpose, so the AI voice reads them correctly.

**Block 1**
In the year two thousand, two men walked into Blockbuster's Dallas headquarters and offered to sell their tiny company for fifty million dollars.

**Block 2**
The company was called Netflix. It mailed DVDs to people's homes, it was losing money, and almost nobody believed in it.

**Block 3**
Blockbuster's chief executive listened politely. One founder later wrote that he was struggling not to laugh. The answer was no.

**Block 4**
Why would he care? By two thousand four, Blockbuster had over nine thousand stores and nearly six billion dollars in yearly revenue.

**Block 5**
But Netflix kept going. No late fees, no trips to the store, then streaming. In two thousand ten, Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy.

**Block 6**
Today Netflix is worth hundreds of billions, and just one Blockbuster is left, in Bend, Oregon. Would you have taken the deal?
