## What it shows

1. **It's a hit-driven page.** A few Reels drive nearly all the views, and the three pinned ones account for about 36M of the 39M. The algorithm tests each Reel, and only the ones people watch to the end and rewatch get pushed further. Many Reels flop, which is normal for this model.
2. **One visual is the whole hook.** One impossible moment is recognizable in the first frame, and there's no talking or text to follow. The same Reel works in every country.
3. **Famous landmarks tell viewers where they are instantly** (Singapore, Rio, London, European old towns) and make them wonder "is that real?"
4. **Short and loopable.** A single 5–15 second clip that viewers rewatch, which is the strongest ranking signal.
5. **Series playlists** (Trampoline Jumps, Cliff Jumping) help people binge and give Facebook's interest survey a clear signal.
6. **Very cheap to make.** Each Reel is one AI clip, compared with the six 15-second segments our Money Stories Reel needs.

## Weaknesses and risks

- **Low pay per view.** With no language, the audience is global and mostly in lower-paying countries. It isn't aimed at US/UK viewers, and viral spectacle clips pay little even at huge view counts.
- **Very easy to copy,** so it will fill up fast. There's no personality or information to keep followers loyal, and interest can drop quickly.
- **AI and policy exposure.** Photoreal people falling from buildings needs the AI label. Content showing dangerous stunts can have its recommendations limited, and "is it real?" comments can attract misinformation reports.
- **Unpredictable.** Most Reels get small numbers, so income swings a lot.

## Lessons for Money Stories (without copying)

- **Put the most striking visual in the first frame.** For example, a lone Blockbuster store glowing under pine trees, or money on fire.
- **Test shorter cuts.** Post 15–30 second "micro-stories" alongside the 60–90 second versions.
- **Pin the top performers** and group Reels into named series playlists.
- **Use landmarks and familiar brand places** as instant context.
- **Post more often.** Views are driven by a few hits, so volume raises the odds of a breakout.
