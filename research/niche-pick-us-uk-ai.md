# Niche Pick: US/UK Audience, AI-Produced, Creator Based in Germany

## The pick: **"Money Stories": true business and money stories**

These are short AI-produced micro-documentaries (45–75 s) about real money and business events:

- **Rise and fall of famous brands** (Blockbuster, Toys"R"Us, Woolworths UK, Kodak, BHS, Sears)
- **"How they made millions"** origin stories (Dyson, Spanx, Greggs, Costco's $1.50 hot dog)
- **Scams and money disasters** (the Fyre Festival, Theranos, Ponzi schemes, lottery winners who went broke)
- **"Why is this so cheap/expensive?"** explainers (Aldi, Primark, Rolex, US healthcare prices)

### Why this niche fits the requirements

| Requirement | How Money Stories fits |
|---|---|
| **US and UK audience** | Nearly every episode can be about an American or British brand, so the content itself draws US and UK viewers. You earn by where **viewers** live, and US/UK viewers pay the most. |
| **Highest pay per view** | Finance and business are among the top-paying ad categories (about $2–5+ per 1,000 Facebook views, compared with $0.50–2 for entertainment). Business case-study content earns some of the highest ad rates among faceless formats. |
| **Viral potential** | It's storytelling, the format with the highest completion on Reels. Brand nostalgia ("I used to go to Blockbuster every Friday!") fits Facebook's 35–65 audience and draws a lot of comments and shares. |
| **AI production** | No face or filming needed. AI voice, AI visuals and motion graphics are enough. |
| **Survives the 2026 originality rules** | *Your own research, script and narration* is exactly what Meta counts as original ("analysis, fresh information, storytelling"). It isn't reposting anyone's footage. |
| **Can't run out of ideas** | Thousands of companies, scams and money events. Easy to repeat as a series. |
| **Location-proof** | Nothing requires living in the US or UK. |

**Backup niche**, if you want more virality and less money: **"Forgotten History / Unbelievable True Stories"**, using the same AI pipeline. It gets more shares but has a lower RPM.

## Niches rejected for this setup

| Niche | Why not |
|---|---|
| Relationship or family skits | AI actors still look uncanny, and the format depends on real people. |
| Faith and motivation | Mostly low-RPM audiences, and the AI template-quote pages that crowd it are the kind of templated content Meta demotes. |
| Cooking, DIY, pets | They only work with real footage, and AI versions get treated as low-effort filler. |
| Pure finance tips | Pays well but gets fewer shares, and it's crowded with "gurus". Money *stories* get shared far more than money *tips*. |

## AI production pipeline (per Reel)

1. **Research:** pick a story and collect 5–8 verified facts with sources. *Fact-checking protects you from misinformation strikes.*
2. **Script (120–180 words):**
   - A 2-second hook: *"In 2004, Blockbuster could have bought Netflix for $50 million. They laughed."*
   - Rising tension
   - A twist or number reveal
   - A final line that loops back to the opening
3. **Voice:** a consistent AI narrator, either American or neutral British English. Use the **same voice every time** so viewers recognize the brand.
4. **Visuals:** cinematic AI images or short AI video clips, plus animated numbers, maps and timelines.
   - Use **stylized** shots, not photoreal fakes of real people.
   - Never make a real CEO appear to say or do things they didn't.
5. **Edit:** 9:16 format, bold burned-in captions (many viewers watch with the sound off), a subtle music bed, and a scene change every 1–2 seconds.
6. **Label:** switch on Meta's AI label. The label doesn't reduce reach, but hiding AI use risks penalties.

## Operating from Germany

- **Language:** English only, with US spelling and US dollar figures for US stories and £ for UK stories. Don't mix German into the Page.
- **Posting time:** **19:00–21:00 German time** (1–3 pm ET, 6–8 pm UK). This hits the US lunch break and the UK evening at the same time. Schedule in Meta Business Suite.
- **Early audience:** your first viewers shape who Facebook shows you to. Don't invite German friends, and don't buy likes. If you ever boost a post, target only the US and UK.
- **Monetization:** you need a Facebook **Page**. Germany is included in the Content Monetization Program, but it's **invite-only**, with roughly a 10k-follower threshold. *Creator Fast Track is US and Canada only*, so you can't use it. The Reels affiliate product links are an extra income option once they're available to you.
- **Admin:** Facebook payouts count as taxable business income in Germany. Registering as self-employed or a business (Gewerbe/freelance) is likely needed, so check with a Steuerberater.

## First 30 days

- **Days 1–3:** create the Page ("Money Stories" or similar), write 20 scripts, and lock in the voice, font and color style.
- **Days 4–30:** post **1 Reel a day**, spread across the 4 formats above. Name them as series ("Brand Autopsy #7").
- **Track:**
  - How many viewers are still watching at 3 seconds
  - Average % watched (target 50% or more)
  - Shares per 1,000 views
  - The **audience country split** (target 60% or more from the US and UK)
- **Day 30:** double down on the best-performing series and drop the weakest.
